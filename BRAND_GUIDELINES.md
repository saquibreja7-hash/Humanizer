## 2. Typography

- **Display & UI Sans:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` — tight editorial tracking (`-0.03em`) on large display headlines.
  - **Signature Two-Tone Headline Pattern:** First words in crisp Espresso Ink (`#1A1311`), emphasized words softly shifted into Warm Taupe (`#7B7069`) — and on Deep Espresso (`#341815`) bands, crisp Ivory (`#F4F1EA`) shifting into Dusty Rose-Taupe (`#A6908B`).
- **Editorial Prose Serif:** `Newsreader` (regular + italic) for live writing previews, editor panes, and literary pull quotes.
- **Metrics & Code:** `JetBrains Mono` for word counts, Fidelity Shield percentages, timing, and API snippets.

---

## 3. Geometry & Surface Craft

- **Buttons:** Crisp architectural corners (`border-radius: 7px`), pairing solid **Oxblood Burgundy (`#7C3539`)** primary CTAs with pure **White (`#FFFFFF`)** secondary buttons.
- **Cards:** Clean `12px–16px` radius with subtle `1px` borders, combining warm limestone/white light cards with rich **Deep Espresso (`#341815`)** contrast cards for high-impact metrics, privacy architecture, and code blocks.
- **Hero Backdrop:** Thin orthogonal SVG architectural flow lines (`#CFCAC0`) with floating square `✳` node chips.
- **Compliance Positioning:** Always maintain clean editorial positioning (*"Natural Writing & Voice Refiner"*) — never use detector-evasion or academic-cheating phrasing in public UI or marketing copy.
